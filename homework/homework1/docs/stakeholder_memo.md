# Stakeholder Memo
**To:** Portfolio Manager  
**From:** Quantitative Analyst  
**Subject:** Stock Market Risk Predictor

## Business Problem

Short-term stock market declines can create significant portfolio losses. Portfolio managers need an early indication of increasing market risk so they can review their exposure before a large decline occurs.


## Proposed Solution

This project will develop a predictive model that uses historical stock market and volatility indicators to estimate the risk of a significant market decline in the near future. The model will generate a simple risk signal, such as Low, Medium, or High Risk.

## Decision Supported

The risk signal will help the portfolio manager decide whether to maintain the current portfolio exposure, reduce equity exposure, or consider additional hedging.

## Key Considerations

The model will rely on historical market data, so its relationships may change during different market conditions. False alarms and missed market declines will also be monitored when evaluating whether the model is useful for real investment decisions.

## Expected Deliverable

A simple decision-oriented report showing the current market risk level, predicted probability of a decline, and key indicators contributing to the prediction.
